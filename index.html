Questions

How does the flight calculator work?

Enter an IATA flight number and date. We look up the operating aircraft and scheduled duration, classify the cabin humidity, and estimate fluid loss from normal daily water output plus extra cabin respiratory loss. The can guide is a journey-length product suggestion, not a prescription.

Why is hydration important when flying?

Aircraft cabins have very low humidity, which can contribute to dehydration. FLYDRATE contains key electrolytes to help support fluid balance while travelling.

When should I drink FLYDRATE?

Enjoy FLYDRATE before boarding, during your flight, or after landing to help replenish electrolytes and support hydration.

Is FLYDRATE only for flying?

The blend is formulated for the dehydration challenges of flying — sodium for fluid balance, potassium for muscle function and cellular hydration, magnesium to reduce fatigue — but it belongs anywhere the journey asks more of you.

What is the FD rating?

FD1 is the most hydration-favourable cabin environment; FD10 is the driest. The score comes from published cabin humidity measurements by aircraft family. It is a cabin-environment rating only — it is not a dehydration score and does not change the fluid-loss calculation. Humidity still varies with airline, load and the specific flight.

How is it sold?

FLYDRATE is available in cases of 12 and 24 cans from flydrate.com, and at UK airport travel-retail units listed on this page. Best enjoyed chilled.

Can I take Flydrate through airport security?

It depends on the airport. Some UK airports now allow liquids up to 2 litres through security, so a 250ml can is fine. Others still apply the 100ml rule — buy airside after screening. Check the Airports list or look up your flight.
